Automatic Plant Fertilizing System Using Arduino-Based Soild Nutrient Sensor

View Project

Soil is composed of macronutrients that directly affect the quality, and the amount of food people eat. In the Philippines, majority of the farmlands where crops cultivate are suffering from low fertility of soils. The main objective of the study is to create a product that will detect these nutrients (nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium) and automatically put organic fertilizer for plant and soil fertilization. This study used an experimental research design and employed quantitative data collection through testing to observe and measure the changes in the variables. Based on the data collected, the automatic plant-fertilizing system provided enough levels of nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium to the sample plant. Additionally, the resulting ratio also indicates that the NPK content of the sample is close to the expected ratio. By also measuring the growth of the sample using the following metrics: plant height and weight, leaf length and width, and root length, the findings have shown that with the application of an automatic plant-fertilizing system, there is a visible increase of growth in the plant.Future researchers will focus on investigating the long-term effects of automated fertilization on soil quality and plant growth to further assess its efficiency and sustainability. As well as analyzing the effectiveness of the automatic plant-fertilizing system across different soil types and various plants to evaluate its adaptability in diverse agricultural conditions.
